Export an MP4 or ProRes 4444 MOV; use --fx-solo for a transparent overlay

Usage: tsrct export [OPTIONS] --project <PROJECT> --output <OUTPUT>

Options:
      --json
          Emit NDJSON progress on stderr and the result on stdout
  -p, --project <PROJECT>
          
  -o, --output <OUTPUT>
          
      --format <FORMAT>
          MP4 for sharing; ProRes 4444 MOV for editing or transparent overlays [default: mp4] [possible values: mp4, prores]
      --resolution <RESOLUTION>
          Output short side: 720p=720, 1080p=1080, 4k=2160 pixels. Upscales smaller canvases; proportional long side rounds to nearest even pixel (ties up) [default: 1080p] [possible values: 720p, 1080p, 4k]
      --fps <FPS>
          Output frames per second as a positive integer [default: 30]
      --start-ms <START_MS>
          Start of the export range in milliseconds (defaults to 0; relative to the solo window with --fx-solo)
      --duration-ms <DURATION_MS>
          Length of the export range in milliseconds (defaults to the remaining duration)
      --start-frame <START_FRAME>
          First source frame at N/fps seconds (relative to the solo window with --fx-solo)
      --frame-count <FRAME_COUNT>
          Exact output frame count; defaults to all frames remaining after --start-frame
      --hdr [<HDR>]
          Encode HEVC Main 10 MP4. Bare --hdr follows videos (HLG if none); pq preserves brighter highlights [possible values: auto, pq, hlg]
      --renderer <RENDERER>
          Scene3D renderer: raster (fast) or path-tracer (slow, physically based light; far slower without a hardware GPU) [default: raster] [possible values: raster, path-tracer]
      --samples <SAMPLES>
          Camera samples per pixel: raster export film/lens samples (default 64) or path-tracer samples (default 128); fewer render faster, noisier drafts
      --max-bounces <MAX_BOUNCES>
          Path-tracer bounces after the first hit, of every type together (default 12)
      --diffuse-bounces <DIFFUSE_BOUNCES>
          Path-tracer diffuse bounces (default 4)
      --specular-bounces <SPECULAR_BOUNCES>
          Path-tracer glossy reflection bounces (default 4)
      --transmission-bounces <TRANSMISSION_BOUNCES>
          Path-tracer bounces through glass and other transmissive surfaces (default 12)
      --volume-bounces <VOLUME_BOUNCES>
          Path-tracer scattering events in a medium (default 0, single scattering)
      --indirect-clamp <INDIRECT_CLAMP>
          Path-tracer largest summed RGB contribution of a sample after the first bounce (default 10; 0 is off)
      --indirect-specular-blur <INDIRECT_SPECULAR_BLUR>
          Path-tracer widening of glossy lobes after diffuse or rough bounces (default 1; 0 is off)
      --no-denoise
          Path trace without the denoiser
      --encoder-backend <ENCODER_BACKEND>
          Encoder implementation used for normal and isolated-FX exports [default: in-process-ffmpeg] [possible values: in-process-ffmpeg, external-ffmpeg-command]
      --ffmpeg-path <FFMPEG_PATH>
          Exact FFmpeg executable to invoke with the external command backend
      --fx-solo <FX_SOLO>
          Export only this composition_id:layer_id over its active window, without audio
  -h, --help
          Print help
