Render one PNG preview frame. For isolated layers use filmstrip --fx-solo

Usage: tsrct preview [OPTIONS] --project <PROJECT> --time <TIME> --output <OUTPUT>

Options:
  -p, --project <PROJECT>
          
  -t, --time <TIME>
          
  -o, --output <OUTPUT>
          
      --hdr
          Write a 16-bit PQ BT.2020 PNG instead of an 8-bit SDR PNG
      --renderer <RENDERER>
          Scene3D renderer: raster (fast) or path-tracer (slow, physically based light; far slower without a hardware GPU) [default: raster] [possible values: raster, path-tracer]
      --samples <SAMPLES>
          Camera samples per pixel: raster export film/lens samples (default 64) or path-tracer samples (default 128); fewer render faster, noisier drafts
      --max-bounces <MAX_BOUNCES>
          Path-tracer bounces after the first hit, of every type together (default 12)
      --diffuse-bounces <DIFFUSE_BOUNCES>
          Path-tracer diffuse bounces (default 4)
      --specular-bounces <SPECULAR_BOUNCES>
          Path-tracer glossy reflection bounces (default 4)
      --transmission-bounces <TRANSMISSION_BOUNCES>
          Path-tracer bounces through glass and other transmissive surfaces (default 12)
      --volume-bounces <VOLUME_BOUNCES>
          Path-tracer scattering events in a medium (default 0, single scattering)
      --indirect-clamp <INDIRECT_CLAMP>
          Path-tracer largest summed RGB contribution of a sample after the first bounce (default 10; 0 is off)
      --indirect-specular-blur <INDIRECT_SPECULAR_BLUR>
          Path-tracer widening of glossy lobes after diffuse or rough bounces (default 1; 0 is off)
      --no-denoise
          Path trace without the denoiser
  -h, --help
          Print help
